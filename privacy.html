Privacy Policy

StarNet Uploader · Effective November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StarNet Uploader ("the application"), operated by Timothy Britt ("the operator", "we"), accesses, uses, stores and shares information when it connects to a Google account and YouTube channel.

1. YouTube API Services

The application uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing the application, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information the application accesses

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Google when you sign in and grant permission.
  • Basic channel information (channel ID and channel title), read only to confirm the correct channel is connected.
  • Video metadata the operator supplies (titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status and publish times) and the video files the operator chooses to upload.

The application requests two scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, to upload videos and set their metadata.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly, used only to read the connected channel's ID and title and to confirm each upload's processing and privacy status.

It does not read your email, contacts, subscribers, comments, watch history or analytics.

3. How the information is used

The information is used only to upload the operator's videos to the operator's channel, set their metadata and publish schedule, and confirm that each upload succeeded. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

OAuth tokens and the upload log are stored only on the operator's own computer, in local files. No data is sent to any server run by the operator; the application talks only to Google's APIs. Video upload records (video ID, title and date) are kept in a local log until the operator deletes them.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any user data with third parties. Data is transmitted only to Google, as required to perform uploads through the YouTube Data API.

6. Cookies and tracking

The application and this website do not use cookies, analytics or tracking technologies.

7. Retention and deletion

You can revoke the application's access to your Google account at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Once revoked, the stored token stops working. To request deletion of any locally stored data relating to you, email the address below; it will be deleted within 7 days.

8. Children

The application is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
